❯ zodiac
Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your machines.

applies to the zodiac iOS app and the zodiac/astrolabe software · august 2026

zodiac (the iOS app) is a remote control for software you run on your own computers. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores, and where:

  • The list of computers you pair — on your device, in the app's own container (shared with its widgets).
  • Pairing tokens — in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Display preferences — on your device.

Where the app connects: exclusively to computers you paired, over your own private network (designed around Tailscale). No vendor servers exist. Terminal content you view travels directly from your computer to your device and is not retained by the app beyond its on-screen session.

Push notifications are sent to Apple's push service by the bridge software running on your computer. Their content comes from — and refers to — your own terminal sessions. You can disable them in iOS Settings at any time, or redact their contents with the bridge's ASTROLABE_PUSH_REDACT option.

Camera access is used only to scan pairing QR codes, when you open the scanner. No images are captured, stored, or transmitted.

Questions? The software is open source — the honest answer to any privacy question is in the code itself: github.com/pr0vId3nce/zodiac.
